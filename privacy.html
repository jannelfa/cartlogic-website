Cart Logic Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026


Introduction

Cart Logic respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when using the application and related services.


Information We Collect

Cart Logic may collect information entered into the application including:

  • Farm names
  • Field names
  • Crop information
  • Cart configurations
  • Product setup data
  • Seeding calculations
  • Saved scenarios

Analytics

Cart Logic may use analytics and crash reporting tools to improve performance, stability, and user experience.

No personally sensitive farming data is sold to third parties.


How Information Is Used

Information collected may be used to:

  • Operate and improve the application
  • Save user configurations and scenarios
  • Troubleshoot technical issues
  • Improve application performance and stability

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information and application data.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.


Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.
