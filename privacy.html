Privacy Policy

Cart Logic Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026


Introduction

Cart Logic respects your privacy.

Cart Logic is designed to store operational information locally on your device. FarmNerd does not maintain a cloud database of your operational information.


Local Data Storage

Cart Logic stores user-entered information required for application features. This may include saved scenarios, product sets, field records, user preferences, and optional seed tag photographs.

This information remains on your device unless you choose to export or create a manual backup.


Manual Backups

Cart Logic does not automatically synchronize your data or create cloud backups.

Backup files are created only when requested by the user and are saved only to the location selected by the user using the device's sharing or file management system.

FarmNerd does not store, retain, or have access to user backup files and cannot recover lost, deleted, corrupted, or damaged operational data.

Users are solely responsible for creating, storing, and protecting their own backup files.


Seed Tag Photographs

Seed tag photographs are stored solely as user reference images. Cart Logic does not read, interpret, analyze, verify, or validate the contents of these images.


Analytics

Cart Logic may use analytics and crash reporting services, such as Firebase Crashlytics, to improve application performance, reliability, and stability.

FarmNerd does not sell user information or operational data to third parties.


Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. FarmNerd does not receive or store payment card information.


Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect application data stored on your device.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.


Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of Cart Logic constitutes acceptance of any updated policy.
